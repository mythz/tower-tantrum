# Replaceable asset contract
All SVG art and WAV audio in this directory are original for Tower Tantrum, generated by `scripts/assets.py` (run from the project root). There are no downloaded art packs, external font/CDN dependencies, or atlases.

Maps are not files: `src/game/presentation/mapArt.ts` builds each theme's 960×576 SVG at startup from the level's lanes and reserved cells (landmarks such as ponds, mesas, lava pools and keeps are drawn on reserved blocks). Changing a route in `src/game/data/levels.ts` automatically updates its art. Build grid: 48×48 cells, 20×12.

Towers: `base-{0,1,2}` pedestals (planter, mossy stone, marble & gold) plus `{pea,berry,frost,zap,sun}-head-{0,1,2}` heads, each 80×80 with the head centred at (40,40) and aiming barrels pointing right (+x); the scene rotates pea, berry and sun heads toward targets. `{kind}.svg` is the composited level-0 portrait used by the DOM cards.

Enemies: `wobbler`, `dasher`, `chunky`, `bubble`, `mender`, `splitter`, `jellet` (80×80) and bosses `captain`, `baron`, `duchess`, `king` (96×96), all facing right with feet near the bottom. Display sizes come from `size` in the enemy definitions. Shields, slows, hit flashes, rage tint, shadows and walking bob are drawn by the scene.

Projectiles and UI: `shot-pea`, `shot-berry`, `shot-frost`, `shot-sun`, `shot-zap`, `icon-bomb`, `icon-chill`, `coin`. Particle textures (glow, spark, chunk, confetti, flake, leaf) are drawn into canvas textures at startup.

SVGs load at the render scale (2× on WebGL) for crisp sprites.

Audio: mono 16-bit 22.05 kHz WAV cues `placed`, `shot-{pea,berry,frost,zap,sun}`, `impact`, `boom`, `pop`, `coin`, `upgrade`, `sold`, `wave`, `boss`, `leak`, `win`, `lose`, `ability-bomb`, `ability-chill`, `heal`, `shield`, `combo`, and a 16-bar 132 bpm chiptune `music` loop. Cues play with slight random pitch variation. Replace with suitable licensed files and change only the manifest URL/extension. Audio failure is optional and silent.

Critical image errors show retry; sounds fail non-fatally. No domain code references asset paths, frames or image dimensions.
